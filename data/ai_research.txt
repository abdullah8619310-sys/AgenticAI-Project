Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science focused on
building systems that can perform tasks which normally require human
intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing images, making
decisions, and learning from experience.

Common AI Capabilities:
- Natural language processing: understanding and generating human language
- Computer vision: recognizing objects, faces, and scenes in images and video
- Machine learning: improving at a task automatically from data, rather
  than being explicitly programmed for every case
- Planning and decision-making: choosing actions to achieve a goal under
  uncertainty

Applications of AI:
- Healthcare: assisting with medical diagnosis and drug discovery
- Finance: detecting fraud and automating trading decisions
- Transportation: powering self-driving cars and traffic optimization
- Customer service: virtual assistants and chatbots that answer questions

AI systems are typically trained on large amounts of data, and their
performance is judged by how well they generalize to new, unseen
situations.
